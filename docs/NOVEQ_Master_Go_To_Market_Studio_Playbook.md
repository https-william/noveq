## 1. /user-research: Empirical Customer Discovery & JTBD Synthesis

### Research Objective & Cohort Sizing
* **Target Audience:** Female undergraduates at Covenant University, young creative and corporate professionals in Lagos (Yaba, Lekki, Ikeja), and verified early buyers.
* **Sample Frame:** 18 in-depth interviews, 6 customer post-purchase calls from initial orders (`ORD-001` through `ORD-006`), and on-campus dorm surveys across Mary, Esther, and Lydia halls.

### 5-Phase Interview Guide
1. **Warm-up (5 min):** Footwear habits, frequency of buying slippers and slides, monthly fashion budget.
2. **Context (10 min):** Daily walking routines, foot fatigue during long campus treks or Lagos commutes, previous experiences with local vs. foreign slides.
3. **Friction Deep-Dive (20 min):** Why previous slides failed (sole detachment, peeling straps, blisters, moisture damage).
4. **Concept Reaction (10 min):** Reaction to NOVEQ Drop 001 prototypes (The Ring, The Weave, The Twist); tactile response to full-grain calfskin vs. synthetic PU leather; price perception at ₦20,000 (introductory) vs. ₦38,000 (full retail).
5. **Wrap-up (5 min):** Sizing preferences, delivery expectations, and payment comfort.

## 2. /mkt-marketing-psychology: Behavioral Economics & Persuasion Architecture

The following cognitive triggers are engineered directly into NOVEQ's copy, checkout flow, and sales dialogues:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│            NOVEQ PSYCHOLOGICAL VALUE STACK             │
├────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────┤
│ Price Anchoring    │ ₦20,000 promo vs. ₦27,000 strike  │
│ Cost-Per-Wear Math │ ₦67 per day across 300 days wear  │
│ Micro-Scarcity     │ Only 1 pair per size in stock     │
│ Effort Signaling   │ Showing hand-stitching & skiving  │
│ In-Group Proof     │ Peer hall sightings & try-ons     │
└────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Price Anchoring & Perceived Value:**
   * In `data/products.json`, every product displays an intentional anchor: The Ring (₦20,000 vs. ₦27,000 compare-at), The Weave (₦20,000 vs. ₦25,000), The Twist (₦25,000 vs. ₦27,000).
   * This anchors the customer on high perceived value while rewarding early adopters with introductory pricing.
2. **Cost-Per-Wear (CPW) Rationalization:**
   * Buying two cheap ₦12,000 PU slides per semester costs ₦24,000 every six months and leaves the customer with broken shoes.
   * Investing ₦20,000 to ₦25,000 in NOVEQ provides 300+ days of comfortable wear, bringing true daily cost to less than ₦70 per day.
3. **Genuine Micro-Batch Scarcity:**
   * Drop 001 is produced in limited mini-runs of 5 pairs per style (only 1 pair per EU size 37 to 41).
   * Displaying exact live stock counts (`stockCount: 1`) triggers immediate action without fake marketing gimmicks.
4. **Effort Justification (The Labor Illusion):**
   * Consumers value products significantly higher when they see the craftsmanship required to build them.
   * Video and photo assets must emphasize raw hides being hand-cut, beveled edges, edge-inking, and perimeter stitching.

## 3. /mkt-marketing-plan: 12-Month fCMO AARRR Growth Roadmap

### Executive Summary
* **Current Baseline:** 6 completed orders, ₦120,000 gross revenue, 100% bank transfer settlement, 83% demand concentrated in The Weave and EU sizes 37–39.
* **90-Day Sprint Target:** Complete sell-out of initial 15 Drop 001 pairs, expand into 50 paid campus orders, and re-anchor price tier to ₦30,000–₦35,000.
* **12-Month Outcome:** Establish NOVEQ as the premier contemporary female slide brand across southwestern Nigerian private universities, Yaba/Lekki young professionals, and diaspora gifters.

### AARRR Funnel Architecture

#### Acquisition (Strangers → Aware)
* **Campus Ambassador Seed Program:** Place 4 sample pairs with influential, respected female student tastemakers in Peter, Mary, and Esther halls at Covenant University.
* **Organic Video Engine:** Daily Instagram Reels and TikTok posts featuring the 9:16 photoshoot footage, tactile leather bending demonstrations, and outfit pairings.
* **Search Authority:** Leverage Google and Bing indexing for high-intent keywords: *"female leather pams in Nigeria"*, *"ladies leather slides Lagos"*, *"contemporary Nigerian footwear"*.

#### Activation (Aware → First Valued Experience)
* **Zero-Friction Campus Delivery:** Free 24-hour hand delivery to dorms with immediate sizing verification.
* **1-Tap WhatsApp Checkout:** Shoppers who hesitate on web checkout can instantly transfer order details to WhatsApp with one tap, where customer support confirms bank transfers and shoe sizing.
* **Legacy Device Safety:** Guaranteed fallback banner on older smartphones (iOS 12.5.7) ensuring older Safari browsers never hit a blank screen.

#### Retention (First-Time Buyer → Brand Loyal Advocate)
* **Leather Care Follow-Up (Day 7):** Automated WhatsApp message checking on fit comfort and sharing leather conditioning tips.
* **VIP Priority Access:** Past buyers receive a private 24-hour early-access link for Drop 002 colorway releases before public announcements.

#### Referral (Customers → Active Advocates)
* **Hall Room Word-of-Mouth Engine:** Offer ₦2,000 discount credits on future drops for every peer referred who completes an order.
* **Unboxing Repost Rewards:** Customers who tag `@noveqthebrand` on Instagram or WhatsApp status receive branded dust bags and care balms.

#### Revenue & Unit Economics
* **Pilot Batch (Drop 001):** Price: ₦20,000–₦25,000 | COGS: ₦12,500 | Gross Margin: 40%–48%.
* **Scaled Public Phase:** Price: ₦38,000–₦45,000 | COGS: ₦14,500 | Gross Margin: 62%–64%.
* **Reinvestment Model:** 60% of all gross margin recycled immediately into bulk raw hide acquisition and improved packaging hardware.
